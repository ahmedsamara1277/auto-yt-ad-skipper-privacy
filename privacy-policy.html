Privacy Policy — Auto YouTube Ad Skipper

Effective date: November 1, 2024 · Last updated: November 1, 2024
Extension name: Auto YouTube Ad Skipper · Version: 1.0 · Publisher: ahmedsamara1277@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Auto YouTube Ad Skipper" browser extension ("the Extension", "we", "us") handles user information when you install and use the Extension. By installing the Extension, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Our Core Promise

The Extension does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information whatsoever. The Extension operates entirely locally within your browser. There are no servers, no databases, no analytics, no telemetry, and no third-party services involved in the operation of the Extension.

2. What the Extension Does

The Extension's single purpose is to automatically skip video advertisements on YouTube. When you watch a video on youtube.com and an advertisement appears, the Extension:

  • Detects the "Skip Ad" button the moment it becomes available on the YouTube page, and clicks it automatically on your behalf; or
  • For "unskippable" ads, fast-forwards the video element to the end of the ad segment by setting video.currentTime = video.duration.

The Extension performs these actions entirely on your local device, in your browser, by interacting only with the YouTube page that is already loaded in your browser tab.

3. Permissions Requested and Why

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions. Each permission is the minimum required to perform the Extension's single purpose:

  • Host permission: *://*.youtube.com/* — Required so the Extension's content script can run on YouTube pages and detect ad-related elements (the "Skip Ad" button and the "ad-showing" player state). Without this permission, the Extension cannot detect ads and cannot function. The Extension does NOT access any website other than youtube.com.
  • Active tab / content script injection — Required to inject the JavaScript that observes the YouTube DOM for ad-related elements. The script runs only on YouTube pages.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

The Extension does NOT collect, access, read, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identity information (name, email, address, phone)
  • Authentication credentials (passwords, tokens, cookies)
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health, medical, or biometric information
  • Personal communications (messages, emails, chats)
  • Location or GPS data
  • Browsing history (the Extension does NOT record which videos you watch, which ads appeared, or which pages you visited)
  • User activity on websites (no keystrokes, no clicks, no scroll tracking, no time-on-page)
  • Website content beyond the specific ad-related DOM elements needed to detect and skip ads (transient in-memory inspection only — never stored)
  • Information about other applications or files on your device

5. How the Extension Operates Locally

When the Extension is installed and you visit youtube.com:

  • A small JavaScript file (content.js, approximately 1 KB) is injected into the YouTube page in your browser tab.
  • This script uses a MutationObserver to watch the YouTube page's DOM for changes.
  • When an ad-related element (such as the "Skip Ad" button) appears, the script interacts with that element locally in your browser — either clicking it or adjusting the video's playback position.
  • All of this happens entirely in your browser's memory. No information leaves your device. No network requests are made by the Extension. No external server is contacted.
  • When you close the YouTube tab or navigate away, the script stops running and any in-memory state is discarded.

6. Third-Party Services

The Extension does NOT use, integrate with, or communicate with any third-party service, API, library, SDK, analytics platform, advertising network, or tracking service. The Extension contains no remote code, no external scripts, and no external resources of any kind. All code is bundled locally within the Extension package and reviewed by Google before publication on the Chrome Web Store.

7. Data Storage

The Extension does NOT store any data. It does not use chrome.storage, localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, cookies, or any other persistence mechanism. When the Extension or the browser is closed, no trace of the Extension's activity remains.

8. Data Sharing and Selling

Because the Extension does not collect any data, it does not share, sell, rent, lease, transfer, or disclose any user data to any third party. The Extension complies fully with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the limitations on use of permissions and the prohibition on the sale or transfer of user data.

9. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the Extension does not collect any personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

10. Your Consent and Rights

By installing the Extension, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Since no personal data is collected, stored, or processed, the rights typically associated with personal data (such as the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data under GDPR, CCPA, or similar regulations) are not applicable — there is no personal data to which these rights could apply.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify users through the Extension's Chrome Web Store listing. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

12. Open Source and Transparency

The Extension's complete source code is contained in the published Extension package and is reviewed by Google before publication. Any user or security researcher may inspect the source code to verify the claims made in this Privacy Policy.
